## Prompt

Create one wide landscape 3:2 editorial illustration asset for Hygeia Indonesian healthcare website, matching a soft hand-painted contemporary editorial illustration with paper texture, realistic friendly Indonesian adult people, refined flat shapes and subtle dimensional shading. Depict a connected-care collaboration as one cohesive scene: in the foreground center an adult Indonesian patient in pale blue casual clothing holds their own smartphone; a physician wearing white coat stands to their left; farther right a laboratory technician in a white coat stands beside a small simple microscope on a bench; a pharmacist in light blue clothing stands beside a shelf holding a few plain unlabelled medicine cartons. Four people total, differentiated by context and clothing, natural proportions, anatomically correct hands. A single graceful cobalt-to-cyan ribbon curves subtly behind the group linking their spaces, never obscuring faces. It represents coordination of care with the patient central, not data freely transmitted. Calm professional warm atmosphere. Restrained palette soft white #F8FBFF, ice blue #E8F3FF, cobalt #0066FF, navy #0A1F44, sparing cyan #00D6FF, natural medium-brown skin tones. Minimal almost white background and generous breathing room. Full characters from waist up with no cropped heads. No text, letters, numbers, logo, labels, arrows, diagram nodes, badges, watermarks, floating UI, charts, medical readings or decorative generic icons. Supporting illustration only, not a website mockup.
